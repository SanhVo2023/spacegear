Identity guidelines · logo · v1.0

spacegear

How to use the spacegear logo. Written for anyone who places it: designers, developers, printers. More dos than don'ts; when in doubt, use the files as they are.

1 · The logo

Idea. A planet split along the dash, its two halves sliding apart: the moment a business shifts gear. Space and gear in one shape: the planet from the site's hero, cut and shifted like a gear change. The cut is the old dash, kept as structure. Like an s, the symbol reads the same upside down.

2 · Construction

One planet of radius R is cut along its equator. The cut is 0.26 R. The halves then slide 0.26 R each way, in opposite directions, so the symbol is point-symmetric. In the horizontal logo one half-planet is exactly one x-height of the name (R = X), the cut runs on the line of the e's crossbar, and the name starts 0.75 X after the symbol. In the stacked logo R = 1.30 X and the symbol sits 0.90 X above the x-height, centred on the name.

The name is drawn, not typed: lowercase on a rounded-rectangle system (x-height 100, stems 20, corner radius 30 on the centre line, vertical cuts), sharing the symbol's language of straight cuts and round corners. Each letter is its own shape.

3 · Clear space

Keep a clear zone of 1 X on every side, where X is the x-height of the name, which is also the height of one half of the planet in the horizontal logo. The zone scales with the logo; never use a fixed distance. In the site's nav pill the pill's own padding is the minimum; nothing else may enter it.

6 · Approved backgrounds

The hero rule. Over the Earth, keep the logo on the nav pill (or another dark surface). Straight on the image, use only the orange logo, and only over dark space or ocean: over bright land and cloud it weakens, and white and two-tone versions vanish. The light-ground rule. On white and light grounds the name goes in Ink (Flame on white is 2.85:1, below the 3:1 floor); the orange symbol may stay as the accent.

7 · Typography

The logo is drawn, not typed. Never recreate it by typing "spacegear" in any font, and never bring back the dash (space—gear). Use the files or the site components.

Interface type. Inter for headings and text (headings use −0.04 em tracking, as on the site); JetBrains Mono for labels, eyebrows and small technical captions. Both are under the SIL Open Font License (licences ship in public/licenses/fonts.txt). The logo itself needs no font licence.

8 · The ™

Show the ™ once, on the footer's giant wordmark, where it is legible, and nowhere else (not in the nav, the favicon or the OG image). It is a claim, not protection: run a professional trademark search on the new mark before relying on it, and switch to ® only after registration (the site's honesty check already rejects ® until then). The ™ is a drawn glyph (spacegear-tm-*.svg), sized to the x-height top, never typed.

9 · Motion

  • The halves slide like the site's curtain. They enter from opposite sides along the cut and lock into the resting logo, in 0.6–0.9 s on the site's ease. On exit they part. The resting frame is always the master.
  • The name rises letter by letter in the footer as the curtain opens (one glyph per element, ~55 ms stagger), and may lean away from the pointer by a few pixels. Glyph order and spacing never change.
  • Never spin the symbol, morph the halves into other shapes, animate the colour, or loop it as decoration.
  • Reduced motion: under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce, show the resting logo with no slide and no rise.
